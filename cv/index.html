Selected publications & preprints

Kerr Quasinormal Modes without Variable Separation: A Two-Dimensional Hyperboloidal Teukolsky Solver with Physics-Informed Neural Networks

Antonio Ferrer-Sánchez, Daniela D. Doneva, José D. Martín-Guerrero, Stoytcho S. Yazadjiev, Roberto Ruiz de Austri-Bazan, Yolanda Vives-Gilabert, José A. Font. arXiv:2608.19774, 2026. Preprint.

Computes the oscillation frequencies of rotating black holes with physics-informed neural networks. A two-dimensional solver keeps the radial and angular equations coupled, without variable separation.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19774

Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Maximizing Quantum Fisher Information in Time-Dependent Many-Body Systems

Antonio Ferrer-Sánchez, Yolanda Vives-Gilabert, Yue Ban, Xi Chen, José D. Martín-Guerrero. arXiv:2604.18506, 2026. Preprint.

Uses physics-informed neural networks to design control protocols that increase quantum Fisher information in driven spin systems. The work connects quantum dynamics and control with improved sensitivity for parameter estimation.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18506

Addressing the gravitational collapse of a massless scalar field with physics-informed neural networks

Antonio Ferrer-Sánchez, Nino Villanueva-Espinosa, Carlos Hernani-Morales, Roberto Ruiz de Austri-Bazan, José A Font, José David Martín-Guerrero, Matthew W Choptuik. Machine Learning: Science and Technology, 2026. Journal article.

Compares neural architectures for scalar-field dispersion and gravitational collapse, including the threshold of black-hole formation. The study examines how network design and adaptive sampling affect accuracy and stability.

https://doi.org/10.1088/2632-2153/ae5459

Trackformers: in search of transformer-based particle tracking for the high-luminosity LHC era

Sascha Caron, Nadezhda Dobreva, Antonio Ferrer Sánchez, José D. Martín-Guerrero, Uraz Odyurt, Roberto Ruiz de Austri Bazan, Zef Wolffs, Yue Zhao. The European Physical Journal C, 2025. Journal article.

Compares transformer-based methods for associating detector hits with particle tracks in simulations for the high-luminosity LHC. It examines reconstruction accuracy and computational cost for sequential and one-shot approaches.

https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-025-14156-3

Gradient-annihilated PINNs for solving Riemann problems: Application to relativistic hydrodynamics

Antonio Ferrer-Sánchez, José D. Martín-Guerrero, Roberto Ruiz de Austri-Bazan, Alejandro Torres-Forné, José A. Font. Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering, 2024. Journal article.

Introduces a weighted physics loss that reduces the influence of steep gradients when learning discontinuous solutions. Tests on relativistic Riemann problems assess how well the method captures shocks and other fluid discontinuities.

https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cma.2024.116906

Physics-informed neural networks for an optimal counterdiabatic quantum computation

Antonio Ferrer-Sánchez, Carlos Flores-Garrigos, Carlos Hernani-Morales, José J Orquín-Marqués, Narendra N Hegade, Alejandro Gomez Cadavid, Iraitz Montalban, Enrique Solano, Yolanda Vives-Gilabert, José D Martín-Guerrero. Machine Learning: Science and Technology, 2024. Journal article.

Learns counterdiabatic controls from physical constraints to suppress unwanted transitions during quantum computation. A two-qubit molecular example demonstrates the joint design of control schedules and adiabatic gauge potentials.

https://doi.org/10.1088/2632-2153/ad450f

Prediction of the risk of cancer and the grade of dysplasia in leukoplakia lesions using deep learning

Antonio Ferrer-Sánchez, Jose Bagan, Joan Vila-Francés, Rafael Magdalena-Benedito, Leticia Bagan-Debon. Oral Oncology, 2022. Journal article.

Investigates deep learning to estimate malignant transformation and high-risk dysplasia from photographs of oral leukoplakia. The pipeline combines lesion segmentation, classification and heatmaps to help interpret its predictions.

https://doi.org/10.1016/j.oraloncology.2022.105967
